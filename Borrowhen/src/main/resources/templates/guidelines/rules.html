Rules

📌 ACCOUNTABILITY RULES
  • Maintain an updated inventory of available disaster equipment.
  • Verify borrower eligibility before approving requests.
  • Ensure proper documentation of all borrowing transactions.
  • Enforce penalties for late returns or misuse of equipment.
  • Post charges for any damaged or missing equipment.
📌 RESPONSIBILITY RULES
  • Provide timely approvals and efficient processing of borrowing requests.
  • Store equipment securely and perform regular maintenance.
  • Ensure borrowers understand lending policies, return deadlines, and penalties.
  • Ensure fairness by prioritizing requests based on urgency and availability.
  • Continuously improve the platform by addressing borrower feedback.
📌 BORROWER RULES
  • Return borrowed items on or before the due date.
  • Handle all borrowed equipment with care and report any damages immediately.
  • Use borrowed items only for their intended purpose.
  • Respect borrowing limits (quantity and duration).
  • Pay penalties for late returns, damages, or losses.
📌 LENDER RULES
  • Provide accurate item details (condition, availability, quantity).
  • Ensure items are in usable condition before lending.
  • Clearly state borrowing terms (return date, penalties).
  • Respond promptly to borrower requests.
  • Track returned items and update inventory accordingly.
